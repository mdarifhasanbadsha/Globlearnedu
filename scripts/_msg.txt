fix: Banglish got an English reply, and "your number" was not recognised

After a full Bangla conversation, "call korte parbo?" was answered in English.
The check was "has Latin letters and no Bangla", which treats Banglish as
English — every Bangla sentence typed in Latin letters failed it.

looksEnglish now requires the message to read as English: any Bangla function
word (korte, parbo, kemon, bhai, taka, lagbe, chai) marks it as Banglish, and
Banglish gets a Bangla reply.

Also widened the call-request patterns: "your number", "give me your number" and
"what is your number" were not recognised at all, so an English student asking
for the number got no call reply.

Verified 5/5: three Banglish phrasings answered in Bangla, two English phrasings
answered in English, all reaching the WhatsApp handover.
